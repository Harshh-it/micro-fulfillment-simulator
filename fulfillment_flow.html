Fulfillment Flow

Each module is a cabinet of storage slots with its own shuttle belt. A shuttle collects every item requested from its cabinet — travelling to the nearest pending slot each time — then hands the batch to the main distribution belt, which routes every item to whichever free station needs the least travel.

Event Log every dispense, jam, merge and pack

Every cabinet slot holds a real, orderable SKU. Each shuttle always moves to whichever pending item is nearest, sweeps up its full batch, and hands it off right at the exit — which doubles as its home dock, since every trip ends there anyway. A new order goes to the free station needing the least total travel, and once a station empties out, a dashed empty cart retraces the route back to that same handoff point.
